Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

TamPH is a personal, single-user tool that creates short-form videos and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not a service offered to other people, and it has no users other than its owner.

What we collect

Nothing. The tool has no server, no database, and no analytics. It runs entirely on the owner's own computer.

What we store, and where

Only on the owner's local machine:

  • An access token issued by TikTok after the owner authorises the tool. It is kept in a file readable only by the owner's operating system account.
  • Video files the tool generates, before they are uploaded.

None of this is transmitted anywhere except directly to TikTok's own API when uploading a video.

What permissions we request

A single scope: video.upload, which allows uploading a video to the authorising account's drafts. The tool cannot read your videos, comments, followers, messages or profile details, and cannot delete or modify anything.

Third parties

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to anyone. There are no advertisers, analytics providers or data processors involved.

Withdrawing access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings, or by deleting the local token file. Revoking access immediately stops the tool from doing anything.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
